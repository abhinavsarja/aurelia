# Supply Chain Note — Rowan Belt
**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Period covered:** 2026-W28 to 2026-W31

## 1. Delayed shipment — Rowan Belt Black (ROW-BT-BLK)

The tannery confirmed on 18 July that the buckle hardware for the
Rowan Belt order is held at customs. The 600-unit replenishment
originally due week 29 is now expected week 34.

Marcus has asked that we do not substitute an alternative buckle,
as the finish would not match the existing range.

## 2. Impact

Rowan Belt is trading behind target for July. No action is proposed
before the shipment clears.
